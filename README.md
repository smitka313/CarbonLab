# Peat Bog Carbon Lab

An interactive simulation for 9th grade biology and Earth science. Students change Earth's tilt, temperature, carbon dioxide, oxygen, the bog's water level and burning, then watch how the rates of photosynthesis, decomposition (cellular respiration) and burning change in an Arctic peat bog.

**Open the simulation:** https://smitka313.github.io/CarbonLab/

## What's inside

- **Opposite reactions:** photosynthesis compared side by side with cellular respiration and burning.
- **The bog:** a live cross-section showing plants, peat, the water level, oxygen in the peat, decomposers, permafrost and fire.
- **Earth's tilt:** how a tilt between 22.1° and 24.5° changes Arctic summer sunlight, permafrost and carbon storage.
- **Zombie fires:** how peat fires smolder under winter snow and return in spring.
- **CO₂ and global warming:** a simple carbon budget and greenhouse model through 2100.

## Student worksheet

`Peat Bog Carbon Lab - Student Worksheet.docx` walks students through the simulator step by step. The teacher answer key is on the last pages; remove them before printing student copies.

## Notes

The rates use a 0–100 scale chosen to show trends. They are not real measurements. The page is a single `index.html` file with no build step.
